Private by design

Your secrets stay yours.

PassVault keeps passwords, secure notes, and two-factor authentication details in a local encrypted vault—without accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync.

A vault, not a service

Useful security without a remote account.

Your vault is stored on your device. PassVault has no application server that receives your passwords, recovery key, activity, or TOTP setup data.

◆

Encrypted locally

Sensitive credential data is encrypted before it is stored, using Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305.

◎

Fast, private sign-in

Use your master password or an optional device biometric. PassVault never receives your Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint template.

Know the boundary

No hidden recovery route.

Because PassVault has no remote copy of your vault, the publisher cannot recover a forgotten master password. Keep a tested encrypted backup and its password in safe, separate places.
