This release delivers a polished local-first PassVault experience.

- Store passwords, secure notes, and other sensitive records.
- Generate TOTP codes and scan setup QR codes on supported mobile devices.
- Use optional biometric unlock, with your master password as the fallback.
- Review password health and organize items with folders and tags.
- Export and restore encrypted `.pvault` backups.
- Includes Arabic and RTL refinements plus stability fixes.
